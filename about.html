About

We made the best one.
Here's why we could.

The letter

I did not set out to make a water bottle. I set out to make the one I wished existed, because everything available was made to hit a price, and I could feel it every time I picked one up. The seams. The weight that was not there. The lid that was fine. I did not want fine.

So we machined instead of molded — from a single piece of steel, the way the good things are made. We spent a year on a cap most people will never think about, because the cap is the whole idea, and a year is what it took to make it hold any phone at any angle without a thought. We made it heavier than it needed to be. Heft is honest; the good things have weight.

It took eighteen versions. Six leaked. Eleven held but the magnet drifted. We did not ship until the eighteenth, because shipping the seventeenth would have meant shipping something that was merely good. I was not interested in good.

This is the result. I think it is the best one anyone has made — not the best value, not the best for any one kind of person. The best. And we will not make a second thing until we can make the best version of that too.

— A.

A. — Brooklyn / Mexico City — May 2026

Where it is made

Every piece of place
we can attribute, we do.

The bottle is machined with our partners in Portugal, in a workshop that has been turning steel for two generations. The cap is assembled in Brooklyn, three subway stops from where the first prototype was drawn. The packaging is printed in Mexico City.

We chose these partners for a plain reason: they were willing to take the orders at our size, and they cared about getting one small thing right. We will name every one of them as soon as the photographs clear.

What is next

Restraint is the flex.

We will not expand the catalog until the bottle has earned it. When we make the next thing, it will be because we can make the best version of it — not because the calendar said to.

No pet bowls. No lunch boxes. No cooler line. We make the best one thing, and then we earn the right to make the next.
