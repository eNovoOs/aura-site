Why it exists

I did not set out to make a water bottle. I set out to make the one I wished existed, because everything available was made to hit a price, and I could feel it every time I picked one up. So we machined instead of molded. We spent a year on a cap most people will never think about. We made it heavier than it needed to be, because the good things have weight. This is the result. I think it is the best one anyone has made.

— A.
