Building the bottle
I wished existed.

I started Aura because I kept buying water bottles that didn't get it. Either they held my phone and looked like gym equipment, or they looked good and did nothing. I wanted one object that did both, the kind I'd actually want to carry to class, to the gym, in the video.

So I'm making it. In public. The cap is the whole idea. MagSafe built in, your phone at eye level. It took 18 versions to get right. Six leaked. Eleven held but the magnet drifted. We shipped the eighteenth, because the seventeenth was only good, and I wasn't interested in good.

You get to watch the whole thing: prototypes, mistakes, the factory, the colorways, the parts that don't work yet. I post it as it happens. If something's not ready, I'll say so. When the first ones ship, the reviews will be real ones, from real people. We won't fake them.

Small on purpose,
for now.

I'm launching small on purpose. A few hero colors first, so the first ones are right and I can actually talk to the people who buy them. The other colorways unlock as the list grows.

I'm not expanding the catalog until the bottle's earned it. When there's a second thing, it'll be because we can make the best version of it, not because a calendar said so.
